Understanding Dholera Smart City (Dholera SIR)

The Dholera Special Investment Region is a planned greenfield industrial and urban city in the Ahmedabad district of Gujarat, roughly 100 km from Ahmedabad. It is a flagship node of the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) and is being developed by the Dholera Industrial City Development Limited (DICDL) under a detailed town planning scheme.

Key infrastructure shaping Dholera's growth

  • Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway: a high-speed link designed to cut travel time between Ahmedabad and Dholera.
  • Dholera International Airport: a new greenfield airport planned to serve the region's industry and residents.
  • Semiconductor and electronics manufacturing: Dholera has been chosen for large semiconductor fabrication investment, bringing high-tech industry and jobs.
  • Solar and renewable energy: large-scale solar park projects in the region support clean, reliable power.
  • Activation Area: the first developed zone with roads, utilities, drainage and smart city systems in place.

Planned infrastructure like this is why investors compare Dholera with how other planned growth corridors in India developed over time. As with any long-term land investment, growth depends on project execution, so we always share the current status of each project honestly.

Is Dholera a good place to invest in real estate?

What documents should I check before buying a plot in Dholera?

Check the 7/12 land record extract, NA (non-agricultural) conversion order, title clearance, the plot's location in the Dholera SIR plan and RERA registration where applicable. Our team helps you review each one.
